## Beyond Stochastic Intelligence

**Author:** Armonti Du-Bose-Hill  
**Type:** Systems governance position paper  

### Thesis

Probabilistic generative systems cannot serve as decision authorities in high-stakes environments.

Decision authority requires:
1. Determinism
2. Replayability
3. Externally verifiable causality

This paper explains why those properties must exist **outside** stochastic inference.

### Relation to Iter

Iter is not the paper. The paper explains why Iter must exist.

Iter implements deterministic governance, replayable decision artifacts, and externally verifiable causality as executable infrastructure.
